Release history

Every push to the release branch is a minor release, tagged automatically from one version file that has to agree with the release commit's subject before the tag exists. How the pipeline does it.

VersionDateWhat shipped
v0.1.0 25 Aug 2026 The site, first release — the hub, its rules, and the pipeline that enforces them. Built from the v0.33.62 brief pack, in the build order the brief specifies. The topic map first, because it is the hub's reason to exist: every non-functional-requirement topic with its position in a line, its canonical source, and its owning site, plus the owns-versus-links contract that keeps a hub from restating its spokes. Then the memory thesis — these sites as a more evolved form of what is usually called LLM memory, with the evidence that it was already the design and the four staleness failures that are its honest limit. Seven owned disciplines, each closing with its counter-evidence rather than a summary: testing and why no-mocks is affordable here and not elsewhere, CI as build-once-verify-before-naming, documentation as a truth system, the methodology published outside the estate for the first time, resilience as design-for-the-failure-you-had, budgets as discipline with every figure deliberately absent, and how a brief becomes work here — the system demonstrated across thousands of documents and described as a system nowhere until now. The honest column ships with the rest rather than later: the scorecard with no plain tick in it, the backups stub published as an absence with the question that has to be answered first, and what this site asserts versus what it can show, including its own unbuilt generators. The two rules the site teaches are enforced on it by CI: a discipline page citing no sibling fails the build, and a page carrying figures without an as-of date fails the build. Plus the agent surface the brief asks for — llms.txt, llms-full.txt, a markdown twin, and the brief pack fetchable at constructed URLs — and the eight source documents published whole, minus the manifest that indexes withheld material.
